# 第4课写实情境图素材

本课四张情境图使用内置 imagegen 生成，人工核对后转为适合机房加载的 1280px JPEG。它们是用于观察与提出问题的 **AI生成示意图**，不是学校现场拍摄、实际控制系统的证据或声学测量结果。学生仍需通过可操作场景验证预测。

| 文件 | 图片要看什么 | 适合的观察追问 | 本课边界 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `public/assets/scenes/road.jpg` | 红色交通信号、等待的行人、斑马线 | 行人获得什么信息？身体作出什么反应？ | 图中只分析行人的反应，不把交通灯自身与行人的判断混成同一个系统 |
| `public/assets/scenes/sound.jpg` | 学生轻拍手、楼道灯亮、附近墙地照亮 | 图中能观察到什么？仅凭一张图能证明灯为何亮吗？ | 不能凭静态图判断声控规则、阈值或是否由拍手触发，需实验验证 |
| `public/assets/scenes/text.jpg` | 键盘、学生输入、屏幕显示“校园” | 学生用什么设备输入？屏幕呈现了什么？ | 输入法内部计算是模型解释，图片不能证明软件实际算法 |
| `public/assets/scenes/door.jpg` | 人接近门、玻璃门打开、门上方模块 | 图中谁在接近？门发生什么变化？ | 静态图无法确定具体传感器或开门逻辑，须在仿真中改变位置检验 |

## 最终生成提示词（内置 imagegen）

- **road**: “Wide landscape 16:9 documentary-realistic photograph of a modern Chinese primary school's pedestrian crossing at the campus gate in daylight. Pedestrian signal clearly RED; a student viewed from behind waiting on the curb with both feet stopped; crossing and safe distance from road. No imminent traffic danger, logos, readable signs, text overlays, arrows or answer labels. Natural anatomy and calm lighting.”
- **sound**: “Wide landscape 16:9 documentary-realistic photo of a quiet Chinese primary school corridor on an overcast afternoon. A student seen from behind lightly clapping near a ceiling-mounted sound-controlled lamp; lamp visibly lit and warm light falling on nearby wall and floor while other hallway areas remain softly dim. No labels, arrows, logos or readable signs.” Final targeted edit replaced a camera-like ceiling device with a small matte-white perforated sound-sensing grille; no video lens remains.
- **text**: “Wide landscape 16:9 over-the-shoulder realistic photo of a Chinese primary school computer room. One student typing Chinese on a physical keyboard; monitor and hands clearly visible; clean document editor and candidate selection panel.” Final targeted edit replaced inaccurate generated UI text with a clean document displaying exactly “校园”, a small “xiao yuan” candidate strip and softly unreadable toolbar.
- **door**: “Wide landscape 16:9 documentary-realistic photo of a modern Chinese primary school library entrance with two automatic sliding glass doors. One student approaching safely from outside; glass door leaves sliding sideways with visible gap and track; a small horizontal sensor module above doorway, no CCTV camera, logos, readable signs, labels or arrows.”

四张原始 PNG 在本任务的 imagegen 输出目录中；项目中的 JPG 是课堂实际使用版本。任何图片失败都不应影响原有3D／2D可操作模型及文字任务。
